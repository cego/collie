A release's **Post for approval** now posts as the human who asked for it, under their own name, instead of as the Collie bot. A request from the bot asks a reviewer to trust a machine. One from a colleague is a normal request.

- Desktop signs the human in to Slack: **Connections › Slack as you** runs OAuth with PKCE and no client secret.
- Desktop renews the sign-in while it runs and gives every Machine `slack-user.env`. **Sign out** removes it everywhere, including from Machines that connect later.
- Each Machine reads a new `slack-user` Connection, and `collie doctor` and `collie connections` report it.
- When it shares, the host checks the token with `auth.test` right before posting. If the sign-in is missing or wrong, it posts nothing, says why, and the human shares the request by hand.
- The approval-channel picker lists the channels the human is in, so nobody has to `/invite @Collie`.
- The design is recorded in ADR-draft-a-message-on-a-humans-say-is-posted-as-that-human.
