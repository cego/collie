When a check left to CI fails and passes where the branch leaves its target, Collie retries its job once. On GitHub, re-running the failed jobs holds back an aggregate job behind `needs` until its needs finish, so the next read found no such job and `ciJob` parked the Run at once as "not in its pipeline", even though a resume 48 s later proved the job green. Now, once a retry is made, `ciJob` reads a `missing` job as pending and waits for it, up to the existing 2-hour CI_BOUND. The change is in `ciJob`, so every forge gets it. A job missing with no retry still parks the Run at once. `docs/cli.md` says so, and a regression test in `test/ci-job.test.ts` fakes a GitHub job that disappears after its retry: it failed on the base and passes here.
