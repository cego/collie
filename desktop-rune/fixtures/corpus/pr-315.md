Stage 1 of the two-stage recovery repairs the `tune-up` workflow, so that a plan it cannot prove ends the Run as **failed** instead of **complete**. run-ff23b932 ended `complete` because a plan-wide 2% tolerance could not be met by its guardrails at the sample count it used. Its own samples show noise and experiment design at fault, not result processing. This PR:

- lets each metric declare its own `threshold`, `tolerance`, `absolute` and `exact` limits, each needing a `why`, judged in `src/measurement-verdict.ts`. A plan that declares none is judged exactly as before;
- classifies every calibration as calibrated, harness broken or proof unavailable. A failed calibration gets at most two bounded repairs, and no experiment is calibrated twice. A repair that only rewords a `why` or spells out an inherited limit is the same experiment, and so is a counter switch back to a plan already tried;
- adds a `from` input that carries an earlier Run's Discover and Harness Outputs and cherry-picks its harness onto the new branch. A harness that an earlier Cleanup took off is put back;
- replays run-ff23b932's calibration in `test/calibration.test.ts`, and adds scenarios for unchanged code, gains, regressions, noisy samples, a broken harness and its repair.
