Six of the open Collie issues, one commit each, with fixups from the review round:

- **#264**: a host replaced by a newer build hands its running checks over to the new host, so a Check in flight survives an upgrade.
- **#265**: an agent can reach a Run its own Run started, or one named in its goal, inputs or a steer, without `--workspace`.
- **#267**: a delivery's unsettled outcomes are recorded once per Run, not again on each replay.
- **#240**: an answer is handed to a Run only once the Run waits on the question. This fixes master's early-answer flakes at the root rather than in the scenarios' helper. Root causes are in the Run's `handoffs/04-master-flakes-root-causes.md`.
- **#266**: a ticket's speed-up criterion is proved by the host's Measurement, `collie measure`, against a benchmark plan named in the ticket, not by hand-timed A/B runs.
- **#273**: tune-up asks whether its progress is enough before it prepares a release. It archives its Measurements and harness patches, and drops back to its base tree on a No.

Ticket 07 (#240's CI half, the `test` gate going from 3 to 6 shards) is **not in this PR**. Changing `.github/workflows/ci.yml` needs a token with the `workflow` scope. The patch and its sizing note are in the Run's `handoffs/ci-test-gate-six-shards.patch` and `handoffs/ci-test-gate-faster.md`, to push as a PR of their own.
