## What the branch does

- **Host:** records are read from a per-Run model with revisions, on a door thread of the host's own. Interactive reads go before background passes. The door reads its first Runs' records before anyone opens one.
- **Desktop:** a Machine's records come over a bridge of their own. The view keeps the records it showed, and keeps them subscribed, so a repeat open paints from what is kept. A record that is not current says so, and its actions are refused.
- **Flow rendering:** an open record is drawn again only when what it shows changes, and only the Steps that changed. A large Flow draws the Steps in view first. A Flow has one context menu and one tooltip, not one per Step.
- **Bench:** `bench/record-open/` times a click on a card to a usable Flow, adds host-only and host-cold cells, and `summarize.ts` summarises native timing logs. `desktop/README.md` says how to measure a record open natively.
